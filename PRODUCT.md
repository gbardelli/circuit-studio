# CircuitStudio

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

Students, makers, teachers and embedded developers design, program and simulate circuits together. The central workflow is placing realistic parts, connecting pins on a breadboard or in a schematic, then inspecting the simulation.

## Design principles

- Preserve the working canvas and immediate manipulation as the primary experience.
- Show the actual component during placement, with feedback before committing a connection.
- Keep technical names, pin functions and model limitations discoverable and accurate.
- Use familiar editing conventions, individual undo, persistent projects and live presence.
- Keep copy brief and purposeful. Avoid decorative statistics, redundant panels and model jargon in the library.

## Visual direction

The existing Apple and Linear references guide hierarchy and restraint. Use Geist throughout the interface and Instrument Sans only for the approved PWM wordmark. Preserve the dark surfaces, restrained blue selection, realistic SVG components and compact controls already in the application.

## Accessibility

Maintain visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, keyboard editing and reduced motion. Explain polarity and pin functions with text as well as color.

## Constraints

Keep the existing free-service architecture. The interactive educational solver is not a substitute for professional SPICE analysis. Do not claim complete Wokwi hardware compatibility or unrestricted model accuracy.
